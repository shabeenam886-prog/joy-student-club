❓ Frequently Asked Questions

📝 How can I register for a club?

Go to Member Registration and fill in the required details.

👥 How can I view club members?

Go to Members → Member Management → View Members.

💬 How can I submit feedback?

Go to Feedback → Give Feedback and choose the appropriate option.

💡 How can I submit a suggestion?

Go to Feedback → Suggestions → Submit Suggestion.
