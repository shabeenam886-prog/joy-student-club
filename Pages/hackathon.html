🔍 Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

📖 About the Activity

Students learn the basics of Search Engine Optimization and how websites can improve their visibility on search engines.

📚 Topics Covered

  • 🔎 Introduction to SEO
  • 🔑 Keyword Research
  • 📄 On-Page SEO
  • 🔗 Off-Page SEO
  • ⚙️ Website Optimization

🌟 Benefits

Students gain useful digital marketing skills and learn how to improve the visibility and reach of websites.
