💻 Hackathon Preparation

🤔 What is a Hackathon?

A hackathon is an event where students work together to create innovative solutions using technology.

📋 Preparation Tips

  • 💡 Choose a suitable project idea.
  • 👥 Form a team and divide responsibilities.
  • 📝 Plan the project before coding.
  • 🛠️ Prepare a working prototype.
  • 🎤 Practice the final presentation.

🌟 Skills Required

💻 Programming, 🧩 problem solving, 👥 teamwork, 🎨 creativity and 🎤 presentation skills.
