🏃 Athletics

📖 About the Activity

Students can participate in athletics activities to improve their fitness, speed, strength and endurance.

🏅 Activities

  • 🏃 Running
  • 🦘 Long Jump
  • ⬆️ High Jump
  • 🏃‍♂️ Relay Races
  • 💪 Fitness Training

🌟 Benefits

Athletics helps students develop physical fitness, discipline, confidence and sportsmanship.
