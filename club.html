Welcome to Student Club Management System

A Student Club Management System is a platform designed to help schools and colleges organize and oversee their student clubs more efficiently. It allows administrators and club leaders to manage club registration, member details, event scheduling, and attendance tracking in one place. By centralizing these tasks, the system improves communication, encourages student participation, and ensures smooth coordination of activities.
