🎸 Rock & Metal

Students explore rock and metal music through singing, instrumental practice and group performances.

🎶 Activities

  • 🎸 Rock Music Practice
  • 🤘 Metal Music Sessions
  • 🥁 Guitar and Drum Practice
  • 🎤 Band Rehearsals
  • 🎵 Live Performances

Members can develop musical skills, teamwork, confidence and stage performance experience.
