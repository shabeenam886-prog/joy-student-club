🧹 Clean Campus Campaign

📖 About the Campaign

Eco Club members organize clean campus activities to promote cleanliness and responsible waste management.

🌿 Activities

  • 🧹 Campus Clean-up Drives
  • ♻️ Waste Collection
  • 🚯 Plastic Reduction Awareness
  • 🗑️ Waste Segregation
  • 📢 Cleanliness Awareness Programs

🌟 Benefits

The campaign encourages students to keep the campus clean and develop environmentally responsible habits.
