🌳 Tree Plantation Drives

📖 About the Activity

Eco Club members participate in tree plantation activities to promote a greener and healthier campus environment.

🌱 Activities

  • 🌳 Planting Trees
  • 🌿 Campus Gardening
  • 💧 Plant Care
  • 🌍 Environmental Awareness Programs

🎯 Objectives

The activity aims to increase greenery, protect the environment and encourage students to take responsibility for nature.
