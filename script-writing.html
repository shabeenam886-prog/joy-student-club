✍️ Script Writing Sessions

📖 About the Sessions

Students learn how to develop creative scripts for dramas, stage performances and short plays.

📚 Topics Covered

  • 📖 Story Development
  • 👤 Character Creation
  • 💬 Dialogue Writing
  • 🎬 Scene Planning
  • 📝 Script Formatting

🎭 Activities

Members work together to create scripts and practice presenting their ideas through drama.
