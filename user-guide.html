📘 Student Club Management System - User Guide

1. 🔐 Login

Enter your username and password to access the system.

2. 🏫 Clubs

Use the Clubs menu to explore different student clubs and their activities.

3. 👥 Members

Register members, add members, view members, record attendance and participation.

4. 🎉 Events

Explore upcoming events and view important event details such as date, time and venue.

5. 🏆 Achievements

View club achievements, awards, competitions, certificates and student recognition.

6. 📚 Resources

Access study materials, e-books, club guidelines and useful links.

7. 💬 Feedback

Submit feedback and suggestions to help improve the Student Club Management System.

8. 🆘 Help & Support

Use the Help & Support section to view FAQs, the user guide, technical support and contact information.
